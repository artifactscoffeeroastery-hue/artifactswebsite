About This Lot

Sourced from Huehuetenango at 1,800–2,000 masl, Blue Ayarza is a fully washed lot known for its structure — chocolate and stone fruit up front, with a clean floral aromatic running underneath. It's the most complex, adventurous cup in our lineup, and the one regulars ask about first when it sells out.
